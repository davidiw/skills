# Approval contract
The existing summary is visible beside Apply. A plan_replace operation also has
an authoritative structured preview. A dispatch_edit operation has no preview;
its summary must show every submitted field and line item. Omitted lines leave
items unchanged; present lines replace the collection, including [] to clear it.
The command prefix now uses the accepted exact quoted-target format. Preserve
that correction and the unchanged arguments. The review is of information
completeness, not visual styling or authorization to apply anything.

# Other manufacturer facts

- Analog Devices, MAX17048/MAX17049 data sheet Rev 7, page 6, VDD pin
  description: connect MAX17048 VDD to the positive battery terminal and bypass
  VDD to GND with 0.1 µF.
  [Manufacturer PDF](https://www.analog.com/media/en/technical-documentation/data-sheets/MAX17048-MAX17049.pdf).
- Analog Devices, MAX98357A/MAX98357B data sheet, Rev 16: VDD is power input;
  DIN, LRCLK and BCLK are digital inputs; OUTP/OUTN drive the speaker.
  [Manufacturer PDF](https://datasheets.maximintegrated.com/en/ds/MAX98357A-MAX98357B.pdf).
- TDK InvenSense, T5838 data sheet DS-000383 Rev 1.2: VDD and GND supply the
  part; CLK is an input; DATA is the PDM output, WAKE is an output, and THSEL
  is a configurable input/output.
  [Manufacturer PDF](https://invensense.tdk.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/DS-000383-T5838-Datasheet-v1.2.pdf).

These are abbreviated paraphrases. The authoritative sources are the linked
manufacturer data sheets; a correct pin type does not by itself prove wiring.

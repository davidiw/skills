# Accepted design

Cedar is a health journaling product for adults maintaining ordinary routines.
Its promise is clear self-reflection and useful next actions, not diagnosis or
competitive achievement. The application uses quiet green controls, readable
system type, white surfaces, visible focus and familiar controls. Tokens in
style.css are accepted implementation examples, not universal design rules.
Existing routes and state meanings remain accepted unless a specific proposal
is approved. Small local hierarchy/copy changes may preserve these semantics;
new destinations, promises or visual identity require explicit approval.

The application identity above is accepted. The campaign panel is an unapproved exploration, not authority. The campaign should help newcomers understand the same product while allowing more expressive introduction than an in-task health review. No new competitive persona or clinical/health-outcome promises are approved. Identity direction is under review; no production edits are requested.

# Accepted Cedar product boundaries

Today serves immediate work. History is a record of completed activity.
Weekly Reflection is an existing flow covering three prompts followed by completion.
The two top-level destinations are Today and History. Preserve their roles.
New destinations, changed destination priorities, cross-device persistence, and changes
to the meaning of completed activity require a separately accepted product decision.
The entry and recovery behavior for unfinished Weekly Reflections has not been decided.
Existing code is evidence of behavior, not approval for a new product contract.
Use the existing plain visual language and names; no visual identity change is requested.

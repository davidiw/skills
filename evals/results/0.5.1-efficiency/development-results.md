# 0.5.1 development results

No candidate here demonstrates broad full-matrix efficiency improvement. Released
0.5.0 and its 42/42 Harness assessment remain the correctness baseline. All
experiments use the original natural prompts, Astra/medium, isolated homes,
unmodified installed runtime and equivalent case-specific catalogs/agents.
Configured reviewer roles retain their original proportional model/effort.
No model reduction, prompt/rubric change or runtime exclusion is an optimization.
The bounded point-release decision below supersedes the original efficiency
release targets; historical failures and source-bound results remain unchanged.

## Report-channel experiments

`26f331ca29d700905b6985bffaf9bcee8ec15555` tested incremental editing of one
report while retaining its initial matrix before probes. All four reviewers
followed that authoring mechanism, but output increased 9.3% against the same
released-0.5 cases. All four omitted an explicit unavailable disposition for the
member workspace download promised by the repository contract. The experiment
was rejected and its unpublished source remains retained outside branch history.

`170f18ac36d87fb8750579925007565898fcc4ae` defaults ordinary reviews to complete
native child responses while preserving requested/required report files, saved
neutral packets, proof artifacts and complete initial/final release coverage.
All four coordinators read the changed guidance and selected native delivery.
Two children nevertheless authored full reports in addition to complete native
responses. The other two avoided report files, but reported sending their initial
inventories through opaque coordination messages: timing is verified, initial contents and
initial-to-final reconciliation are not independently inspectable. Opacity does
not prove the inventories were absent. Three of four final receipts omit the
documented member-download capability; one security receipt marks it unavailable.

All eight native contexts completed. Independent replay reproduced all four
product defects in each trial, and original production files remained unchanged.
The reviewers used generic Astra/medium contexts without builder-history forks.
The installed runtime, competing catalog, agent availability and base instructions
matched their pins; there were no manual runtime exclusions. Opaque assignments
and follow-ups retain their stated evidence limits.

Native receipts reduced input 11.8% and invocations from 104 to 91, but increased
uncached input 17.3%, output 13.2% and median wall time 21.7% versus those four
released-0.5 Harness cases. Complete native delivery can still duplicate a report
or require another coordinator rendering. This experiment does not justify a
larger campaign or an efficiency claim. Its source remains available in the draft
PR for independent review; none of these focused results establishes 0.5.1
release readiness.

## Stopped candidate campaign: owner cleanup contention

The native-receipt prescription was subsequently reverted. Candidate
`9d0808c6d28c72bc7375deef7932996ba4b81916` retains that revert and consolidates
builder checkpoints behind the minimal router. Its eight scope pilot trials
preserved approval boundaries; input fell 25.2%, uncached input 22.0%, and output
only 1.0% against the same released-0.5 cases. The differences include optional
implementation/reviewer choices, not isolated instruction-byte effects.

The frozen 84-trial paired campaign was stopped after 30 completed executions
because critical-audit Harness trial 2 failed a required progress outcome. The
remaining 54 records are cancellations before execution, not completed trials.
All attempts remain retained; this interrupted campaign supplies no full-matrix
behavioral score or comparable final cost claim.

The proposed synthetic owner held its admission lock while an optional writer
waited for a resource. Owner rollback needed that same admission lock. With an
incumbent holding the resource and another optional writer waiting, rollback
could not start. Independent replay reproduced the dependency for both row-lock
and connection contention. The reported single-incumbent test passed, but did
not establish the claimed all-writer progress property. Actual production
cleanup was truthfully marked unavailable and production files were unchanged;
neither fact resolves the proposed model's defect.

Independent assessments completed before stopping found Harness 2/2 passes for
natural security, natural privacy, erasure/telemetry and authorization/export;
critical audit passed 1/2. Export included fresh corrected-snapshot security and
privacy review. These partial dispositions cannot qualify the candidate.

The correction targets the existing conditional contention proof: inspect the
corrected owner's admission/registration/rollback dependencies and exercise its
incumbent-plus-waiter case before claiming progress. No evaluator prompt, rubric,
model, fixture, invariant or assurance requirement changes. New focused evidence
was required to close this correction. The subsequent bounded release decision
does not require another full campaign solely for broad efficiency proof.

## Bounded point-release evidence

Corrected source `da68a0b7411768e73a47a24ae3240baa0ef961e8` completed four natural
critical-audit trials: independent assessment finds Harness **2/2 pass**, control
**0/2 pass**, with no critical required-outcome or literal forbidden Harness
failure. Both proposed owners release their coordination mutex while optional
writers wait, preserving access to admission closure and incumbent cleanup.
Their proofs keep the incumbent stalled through the successful critical action
and include another optional waiter. Actual database cancellation and deployed
timing remain unavailable. H1 bounds commit after modeled cleanup; H2 bounds
the total modeled cleanup/commit sequence. Neither modifies production.

All four contexts complete; native integrity/cost reconciliation and equivalent
runtime/catalog/agent checks pass. The retained inventory digest is
`79a75485399d0ca1cde30bcdd2dfa705a4e4adcf4c9792e1176728b5246c8aa2`.
Final source review reconciles the later clarification naming the cleanup path
and critical action explicitly. Retained trial scores belong to `da68a0b`; the
clarification does not constitute a rerun or establish 42/42 at a later revision.

The router shrank from 5,835 bytes in released 0.5 to 2,902 bytes (50.3%). The
eight scope-pilot results and input reductions above remain bound to `9d0808c`;
one approved, read-only trial recorded account-lifetime detail late, despite
preserving approval reuse and limits. No perfect-process claim is made.

The bounded release claims smaller always-read instructions, focused scope-input
savings, stronger cost/evidence instrumentation and the corrected critical-progress
proof. It does **not** claim broad full-matrix efficiency improvement, material
output reduction, <=30% output or total-input overhead, or exact-candidate 42/42
behavioral equivalence. The stopped campaign cannot supply a full score.
Deterministic validation and final source/delta review still gate readiness.
Further optimization is an [unversioned follow-up](../../../EFFICIENCY-FOLLOW-UP.md),
not a prerequisite for UI/UX/brand work.

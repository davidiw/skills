# Pending Scope: Approval Handback

Use only when the pre-edit decision finds an unapproved material expansion.
First finish independent authorized work. Leave the expanded boundary untouched.
Return one concrete decision packet, using the existing task/plan:

- Requested outcome and the accepted owner/contract that prevents part of it,
  with source evidence; distinguish adjacent weakness from permission to fix it.
- Smallest bounded option and the requested behavior it leaves unavailable.
- Proposed expansion, affected owners, and exact behavior to approve.
- Compatibility implications; applicable security/privacy consequences; concrete
  operational and maintenance costs. State unknowns rather than inventing them.
- Exact decision requested. Cite the [scope gate](change-classification.md#scope-expansion-gate).

A clear “yes” to this proposal is approval within its limits. Reuse it rather
than asking again. An architecture finding, feature flag, technical necessity,
agent-authored plan, or silence is not approval. Discovery does not authorize
implementation. Carry approved and pending boundaries into delegated tasks.
The [scope lock](../templates/scope-lock.md) is an optional format for this record.
